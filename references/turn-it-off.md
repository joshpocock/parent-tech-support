# Have You Tried Turning It Off Module

Run these in order, one step per message when talking to the parent. Stop as soon as it works.

1. **Is it charged and plugged in?** Look for the battery symbol. Plug it in and wait two minutes.
2. **Is it on the internet?** Look for the Wi-Fi fan symbol at the top of the screen. If it is
   missing, check the router has its lights on.
3. **Close the app that is stuck.** Phone: swipe it away from the recent apps view. Computer:
   save work, then close the window.
4. **Restart properly.** Not just the screen going dark:
   - iPhone or iPad: hold the side button (and a volume button on newer models) until "slide to
     power off" appears, slide it, wait 30 seconds, hold the side button until the Apple logo shows.
   - Android: hold the power button (or power and volume up on some Samsungs) and choose Restart.
   - Windows: Start, the power symbol, Restart (not Shut down).
   - Mac: the Apple menu at the top left, Restart.
   - Smart TV or router: unplug it from the wall, count to 30, plug it back in, wait two minutes.
5. **Updates.** If it says an update is waiting, let it finish while plugged in.
6. **Still broken?** Write down exactly what the screen says (or take a photo) and stop there.
   That photo is what the helper needs.

Never at this stage: factory reset, deleting apps they use, or anything that erases photos or
messages. Those need the helper present.

Menus change between versions. If the device does not match these words, check the maker's
current help page (with web search) before guessing.
