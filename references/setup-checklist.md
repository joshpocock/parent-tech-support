# New device setup checklist (phone, tablet or computer)

Do this with the parent, on their device, with them pressing the buttons where possible. Check
each step against the maker's current help page; menu names change between versions.

## Make it readable

- [ ] Larger text (Settings, Display or Accessibility, Text Size).
- [ ] Bold text and higher contrast if it helps.
- [ ] Louder ringtone and vibration on; flash for alerts if hearing is an issue.
- [ ] Fewer apps on the home screen: phone, messages, camera, photos, the two or three they use.

## Make it safe

- [ ] Automatic updates on.
- [ ] A screen lock they can remember (a passcode they choose; Face ID or fingerprint as well).
- [ ] Silence or screen unknown callers (iPhone: Silence Unknown Callers; many Android phones:
      spam call filtering or Call Screen in the Phone app settings).
- [ ] Find My (Apple) or Find My Device (Google) switched on.
- [ ] Backups on (iCloud or Google One), and a check that they finished.
- [ ] Their passwords in the device's built-in password manager, or written in a paper book kept
      at home and never photographed. You never need to know them.
- [ ] Two-step verification on their email account, with your number as a backup only if they agree.

## Make it findable in an emergency

- [ ] Emergency contacts and Medical ID (iPhone) or Emergency information (Android).
- [ ] You saved as a favourite contact with a clear name, for example "Sam (son)".

## Leave behind

- [ ] A Big Font Mode guide for the two things they do most.
- [ ] The scam rule on the fridge: hang up, call back on a number you already have.
- [ ] A "call me only if" list.
